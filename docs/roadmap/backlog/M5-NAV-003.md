# Adaptive navigation coordination

## Scope

Coordinate window-size breakpoints, navigation variant transitions, state preservation, and
package/namespace decisions across adaptive components.

## Exit criteria

Breakpoints and transitions are documented, deterministic layout tests pass, and the package
boundary is consistent with D001.

## Delivered contract

- D014 adds `MaterialNavigationSuiteScaffold` and `NavigationSuiteLayoutType` to the existing
  `Shirasagi0012.Material3Expressive.Avalonia` assembly and `Material3Expressive.Controls`
  namespace. Breakpoint calculation remains internal as required by D001.
- Auto layout applies the pinned AndroidX preferred rule at the exact 600dp width and 480dp height
  boundaries. Explicit compact, medium, collapsed-wide, expanded-wide, and none layouts support
  application-owned posture or policy decisions without a desktop-global posture service.
- One collection of real `MaterialNavigationItem` instances moves between one short bar and one
  wide rail. Selection, enabled state, item automation peers, and the focused destination survive
  the transition; compact/collapsed and medium/expanded item geometry follows the corresponding
  upstream types.
- Horizontal navigation occupies the bottom edge, vertical navigation occupies logical start,
  and RTL mirrors that placement. `PrimaryAction` is 16dp above a bar and becomes the rail header.
  The body consumes the remaining area and fills the scaffold for `None` or a settled hidden
  navigation component.
- `IsNavigationVisible` uses a reversible DefaultSpatial spring with read-only progress.
  Automation adds only a neutral Pane parent and retains the active Tab/TabItem subtree.

## Upstream and executable parity

The implementation baseline is AndroidX `ff0a0a99`
`material3-adaptive-navigation-suite`, specifically `NavigationSuiteScaffold.kt`,
`WindowSizeClassHelper.kt`, `NavigationSuiteScaffoldTest.kt`, and
`NavigationSuiteScaffoldDeviceTest.kt`.

The pinned Compose Multiplatform alpha22 producer has no separate adaptive-navigation-suite
artifact. Its ShortNavigationBar also has the recorded full-width-per-item measurement defect, so
compact and medium executable parity are unavailable. The collapsed-wide endpoint can be composed
from its executable `WideNavigationRail`; light and dark suite cases pass the existing comparison
profile as `semanticRasterEquivalent`, with exact structural contracts and zero rejected pixels,
at `artifacts/visual-parity/xunit/20260730-191815-054`. No tolerance, warning, or comparison
profile changed.
